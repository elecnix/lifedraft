# KOHO Account Benefits - Large Balance Analysis

## Overview
Investigating benefits of holding up to $500,000 (full CDIC insured amount) in KOHO checking account.

## CDIC/SADC Protection

### Coverage Details
- **Maximum Coverage**: $100,000 CAD per depositor per institution
- **For KOHO Save Accounts**: Up to $100,000 CAD per named beneficiary in the trust
- **Protection Agency**: Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation (SADC in French)
- **Protection Type**: Free and automatic - no enrollment required
- **What's Covered**: All eligible deposits including checking, savings, and term deposits

### For Half Million Dollars ($500,000)
Since CDIC covers a maximum of $100,000, holding $500,000 would mean:
- **Fully protected portion**: First $100,000 USD/CAD
- **Unprotected portion**: Remaining $400,000
- **Risk**: In event of KOHO's insolvency, only the first $100,000 would be guaranteed

**Recommendation**: For balances approaching or exceeding $100,000, consider:
1. Spreading funds across multiple CDIC-insured institutions
2. Using KOHO for daily spending and cash back benefits, while keeping larger sums in traditional banks with CDIC coverage

## Considerations for Large Balances

### Protection Strategy
For $500,000 balance:
- Keep $100,000 in KOHO (fully insured)
- Remaining $400,000 in other CDIC-insured institutions for full protection

### Important Distinction: KOHO Save vs Regular Balance
- **KOHO Save**: Earns 3.5% interest - this is the protected, interest-bearing portion
- **Regular KOHO Account**: Base spending account
- Interest rate applies to funds in KOHO Save vault

### Alternative Approach
Use KOHO for:
- Daily spending account (earning 1% cash back)
- Emergency fund portion ($100,000 insured limit in KOHO Save)
- Keep remainder in traditional savings/investment accounts

### Liquidity Considerations
- Funds are accessible via Interac e-Transfer, ATM, or debit card
- Interest continues to accrue in KOHO Save
- No withdrawal penalties (unlike GICs)

## KOHO Save Specifics

### How KOHO Save Works
- Separate vault within KOHO account
- Funds can be transferred between Save and spending instantly
- Interest earned on daily closing balance
- No lock-in period - withdraw anytime

### Eligible Funds
- Regular deposits into KOHO Save
- Direct deposits can be split to Save
- Money from spending account can be moved to Save

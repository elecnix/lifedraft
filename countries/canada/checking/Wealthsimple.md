# Wealthsimple Chequing Account - High Balance Benefits ($500K+)

## Overview
Wealthsimple Chequing accounts are offered by Wealthsimple Investments Inc. (WSII) and Wealthsimple Payments Inc. The chequing funds are ultimately held in trust with multiple CDIC-member institutions. This strategy for high balances leverages the unique trust structure for enhanced CDIC coverage.

**Data Update**: May 13, 2025 - All figures and comparisons are based on data from this date.

## CDIC Insurance Coverage Structure

### Protection Details
- **Aggregate Maximum Coverage**: $1,000,000 CAD per account holder
- **Per Institution Limit**: $500,000 CAD per beneficiary, per CDIC institution member
- **Distribution Requirement**: Funds must be distributed between at least ten (10) CDIC-member institutions to achieve the $1M maximum coverage
- **Trust Structure**: Funds held in trust for the primary account holder through multiple partner institutions
- **Scope**: Covers eligible cash balances in chequing accounts

### Account Providers
- **Wealthsimple Investments Inc.** (WSII) - OSC regulated investment dealer
- **Wealthsimple Payments Inc.** - FINTRAC registered Money Services Business
- Neither WSII nor Wealthsimple Payments Inc. are CDIC members themselves

### Key Legal Language (May 2025)
> "Le solde de votre compte chèques Wealthsimple est détenu en fiducie pour vous auprès de plusieurs institutions membres de la Société d'assurance-dépôts du Canada (SADC). En vertu du cadre fiduciaire, la SADC assure les soldes en espèces admissibles jusqu'à concurrence de 500 000 $ par bénéficiaire et par institution membre."

## Exclusive High-Balance Benefits

### 1. ATM Fee Reimbursement ($5/tx cap)
- **Reimbursement Policy**: Unlimited ATM fees reimbursed up to $5 per transaction
- Reimbursement processed within 4 business days to the associated Mastercard account
- Annual savings potential for frequent travelers: $1,200-$3,600+

### 2. No Foreign Exchange Markup
- **FX Policy**: Wealthsimple charges 0% foreign exchange fees
- Note: Card networks and merchants may still impose standard conversion fees
- International spending advantage vs 2-2.5% FX fees at traditional banks

### 3. Elevated e-Transfer Limits
- **Daily Limit**: Up to $25,000 for eligible clients (vs $5K-$10K at competitors)
- Determined by: total Wealthsimple assets, direct deposit amount, transaction history
- Essential for large property transactions and business payments

### 4. No Monthly Account Fees
- **Fee Structure**: $0 monthly fees, no minimum balance requirements
- Contrast: Major banks charge up to $30/month

## Strategic Considerations for $500K-$1M Balances

### CDIC Distribution Strategy
To achieve full $1M coverage, funds must be distributed across at least 10 CDIC institutions. This happens automatically through Wealthsimple's trust structure, but the $500K/$institution limit is critical to understand for:
- High-net-worth individuals storing significant cash reserves
- Property purchase bridge funding
- Corporate treasury management

### Interest Rate Risk
- Rates fluctuate with Bank of Canada policy
- Current high-rate environment makes this strategy optimal
- Monitor for rate decreases and adjust cash allocation accordingly

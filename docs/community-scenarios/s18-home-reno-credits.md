# S18 — Multigenerational / accessibility renovation credits

- **Community source:** r/PersonalFinanceCanada threads on caring for aging parents;
  canada.ca Multigenerational Home Renovation Tax Credit
  <https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/deductions-credits-expenses/multigenerational-home-renovation.html>
  and Home Accessibility Tax Credit
  <https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/deductions-credits-expenses/line-31285-home-accessibility-expenses.html>.
- **Program / maneuver:** A family of credits for aging-in-place — the Multigenerational
  Home Renovation Tax Credit (15% of up to $50,000 to build a secondary unit for a
  senior/adult with a disability), the Home Accessibility Tax Credit (15% of up to
  $20,000), and the Canada Caregiver Credit.
- **Situation:** A household builds a secondary suite so a parent can move in, claiming
  the refundable MHRTC on up to $50,000 of costs and stacking the HATC on accessibility
  renovations, while the caregiver credit offsets support costs.
- **Why it is interesting (simple, relatable, timely):** Directly tied to a common life
  event (aging parents, "sandwich generation") with clear caps — an easy, marketable
  scenario the engine could quantify.
- **Engine coverage — GAP.** No home-renovation-credit logic exists (`grep -ri
  "multigenerational\|home accessibility\|caregiver" countries/canada/` finds nothing).

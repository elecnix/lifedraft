# S14 — Corporate / holdco passive investing and corporate-class funds

- **Community source:** Loonie Doctor, "Investing Through A Professional Corporation"
  <https://www.looniedoctor.ca/2018/08/04/corporation-investing/> and the Money Scope
  podcast "Investing in a Canadian Corporation"
  <https://moneyscope.ca/2024/03/29/episode-10-investing-in-a-canadian-corporation/>.
- **Program / maneuver:** Passive investing inside a CCPC / holding company — tax
  deferral on active income, the passive-income small-business-deduction grind
  ($50k-$150k of passive income), corporate-class funds to defer distributions, the
  Capital Dividend Account (CDA), and salary-vs-dividend integration.
- **Situation:** An incorporated professional retains after-tax active income to invest
  in the corporation, using corporate-class funds to minimize taxable distributions and
  drawing a salary/dividend mix to fund RRSP room and personal spending while managing
  the SBD grind.
- **Why it is interesting (highly complex):** Integration, the passive-income grind,
  CDA, and RDTOH make corporate investing the deepest maneuver for high earners — and a
  major differentiator for a planning engine that could model it.
- **Engine coverage — GAP.** No corporation/holdco account type, no corporate-class fund
  modeling, no SBD-grind or CDA/RDTOH logic (`grep -ri "corporate class\|holdco\|small
  business deduction" countries/canada/` finds nothing relevant). **Roadmap candidate.**

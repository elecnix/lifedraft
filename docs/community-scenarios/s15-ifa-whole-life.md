# S15 — Immediate Financing Arrangement (leveraged whole-life)

- **Community source:** Globe and Mail, "Immediate financing arrangements that leverage
  permanent life insurance grow in popularity"
  <https://www.theglobeandmail.com/investing/globe-advisor/advisor-practice/article-immediate-financing-arrangements-that-leverage-permanent-life/>;
  Canada Life IFA guide
  <https://www.acp.canadalife.com/content/dam/advisors/documents/strategies/tepg/en_ca/articles/ifa/Immediate%20financing%20arrangement%20CL%20En.pdf>.
- **Program / maneuver:** Immediate Financing Arrangement — buy a participating
  whole-life policy, immediately assign its cash value (90-100%) as collateral for a
  bank loan, redeploy the borrowed funds into a business/investment, and deduct the loan
  interest and part of the insurance cost (NCPI).
- **Situation:** An incorporated professional or business owner with strong cash flow
  funds a large whole-life premium, borrows it right back to keep capital working, and
  gains permanent insurance plus interest and NCPI deductions — typically $50k/yr
  premiums for 10 years supporting a $500k+ facility.
- **Why it is interesting (highly complex, advanced):** Combines insurance cash-value
  growth, leverage, interest deductibility, NCPI, and estate/CDA effects; sensitive to
  interest-rate and investment risk. A signature high-net-worth maneuver.
- **Engine coverage — GAP.** No permanent-life-insurance cash-value model and no
  leveraged-insurance/NCPI logic exist (`grep -ri "whole life\|immediate financing\|IFA"
  countries/canada/` finds nothing relevant). **Roadmap candidate (advanced tier).**

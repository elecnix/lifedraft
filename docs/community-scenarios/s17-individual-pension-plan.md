# S17 — Individual Pension Plan (IPP)

- **Community source:** Loonie Doctor incorporation/tax-planning pages
  <https://www.looniedoctor.ca/tax-planning/>; canada.ca registered-pension-plan guidance
  <https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/registered-plans-administrators/pspa/individual-pension-plans-ipps.html>.
- **Program / maneuver:** Individual Pension Plan — an employer-sponsored defined-benefit
  pension for an incorporated owner/professional, allowing higher tax-deductible
  contributions than an RRSP as the member ages, plus past-service buy-backs and
  terminal funding.
- **Situation:** An incorporated professional in their 50s sets up an IPP so the
  corporation makes larger deductible contributions than the RRSP limit permits,
  creating creditor-protected pension assets and shifting income out of the corporation.
- **Why it is interesting (complex, age-dependent):** IPP room grows with age and
  salary, contributions are actuarially determined, and the RRSP-vs-IPP crossover plus
  past-service PSPA calculations are non-trivial — a distinctive high-earner planning move.
- **Engine coverage — GAP.** No IPP/defined-benefit-for-owner model exists (the only
  `IPP` matches in the tree are the word "shipped"). RRSP room is modeled but not the
  actuarial IPP alternative. **Roadmap candidate.**
